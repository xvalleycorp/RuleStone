RuleStone logo concepts

Four replacements for the current stele logo, which reads as a tombstone. Each keeps the basalt-and-engraved-gold palette and avoids any upright slab silhouette. Every mark is hand-written SVG on a 64×64 grid with at most three colours, plus a dark-mode variant. The lockup wordmark is live text in Inter, 'Segoe UI', Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif, not paths. Nothing here replaces the live logo yet.

1. Keystone

An arch of five cut stones held by a gold keystone: the rule that locks the whole system together. It reads as a bridge or gateway of trust, not a slab, and the gold wedge echoes the engraved-gold palette.

  • #44403c stone 700
  • #f59e0b gold 500
  • #d6d3d1 / #fbbf24 dark-mode stone / gold

2. Cut stone

A step-cut gemstone seen from above: lit and shaded facets around a gold table. It says value and precision (many facets, many rules) and stays a bold, solid square at 16 px.

  • #57534e lit facet
  • #1c1917 shaded facet
  • #f59e0b gold table
  • #d6d3d1 / #78716c / #fbbf24 dark-mode facets / table

3. Decision block

A squared stone block in isometric view whose gold top face is engraved with the rows of a decision table. Solid, structured and quietly suggests a gold ingot: something of fixed, verifiable value.

  • #57534e left face
  • #1c1917 right face
  • #f59e0b gold top, grooves #92400e
  • #e7e5e4 / #a8a29e / #fbbf24 dark-mode faces / top

4. Block R

The letter R built from cut stone blocks: a solid upright and stacked courses, like the rows of a decision table; the gold leg strides forward for “every change in seconds”. The most ownable and the most product-like (works as an app icon).

  • #44403c stone 700
  • #f59e0b gold 500
  • #d6d3d1 / #fbbf24 dark-mode stone / gold
